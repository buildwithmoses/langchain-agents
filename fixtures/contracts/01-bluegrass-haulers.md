# BROKER-CARRIER AGREEMENT

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Ridgeline Freight, Bluegrass Haulers LLC and every name, address and number in this document are invented. This is not legal advice and not a real contract.

**Agreement No.** RF-CA-2026-0117
**Effective Date:** March 2, 2026

This Broker-Carrier Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, a Tennessee limited liability company and licensed property broker (MC-000000), with its principal office at 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, Tennessee 38100 ("Ridgeline" or "Broker"), and **Bluegrass Haulers LLC**, a Kentucky limited liability company and authorized for-hire motor carrier (USDOT 0000001, MC-000001), with its principal office at 22 Placeholder Road, Lexington, Kentucky 40500 ("Carrier"). Ridgeline and Carrier are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Shipper" means any person or entity that tenders freight to Ridgeline for arrangement of transportation.

1.2 "Load" or "Shipment" means the goods tendered under a single bill of lading and a single Rate Confirmation.

1.3 "Rate Confirmation" means the written load tender issued by Ridgeline to Carrier identifying the origin, destination, equipment, rate and any special instructions for a Load.

1.4 "Claim" means any claim, demand, suit, loss, liability, fine, penalty, cost or expense, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

1.5 "Applicable Law" means all federal, state and local laws and regulations governing the transportation of property by motor carrier, including 49 U.S.C. § 13101 et seq. and the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations.

## 2. Scope and Relationship

2.1 Ridgeline arranges transportation of property for its Shippers. Carrier agrees to transport Loads tendered by Ridgeline and accepted by Carrier under the terms of this Agreement and each Rate Confirmation. In the event of a conflict, this Agreement controls over any Rate Confirmation, bill of lading or Carrier tariff.

2.2 Carrier is an independent contractor. Carrier has exclusive control over its drivers, equipment and operations and is solely responsible for the employment, compensation and supervision of its personnel.

2.3 Nothing in this Agreement obligates Ridgeline to tender any minimum volume of freight or obligates Carrier to accept any particular Load.

## 3. Carrier Authority and Compliance

3.1 Carrier represents and warrants that it holds, and will maintain throughout the Term, active operating authority from the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, and that it does not hold an "Unsatisfactory" safety rating.

3.2 Carrier shall comply with all Applicable Law, including hours-of-service, drug and alcohol testing, and equipment maintenance requirements. Carrier shall notify Ridgeline in writing within two (2) business days of any change in its operating authority or safety rating.

3.3 Carrier shall not transport hazardous materials under this Agreement.

## 4. Rates and Accessorials

4.1 Rates for each Load are set out in the applicable Rate Confirmation. No additional charges shall be payable unless agreed in writing by Ridgeline before they are incurred.

4.2 Detention is payable after two (2) hours of free time at pickup and at delivery at the rate stated in the Rate Confirmation, provided Carrier records accurate in and out times on the signed bill of lading.

## 5. Billing and Payment

5.1 Carrier shall submit an invoice with a signed proof of delivery for each Load.

5.2 Ridgeline shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt of the invoice and all required documents.

5.3 At Carrier's option, Carrier may elect quick pay, in which case Ridgeline shall pay the invoice within two (2) business days of receipt less a quick pay fee of three percent (3%) of the invoice amount.

5.4 Carrier agrees that Ridgeline is solely responsible for payment of Carrier's charges and that Carrier shall not seek payment from any Shipper or consignee.

## 6. Cargo Liability

6.1 Carrier shall be liable for loss of, damage to, or delay of cargo from the time it is received by Carrier until it is delivered, in accordance with the Carmack Amendment, 49 U.S.C. § 14706.

6.2 Carrier's liability shall be for the full actual value of the cargo, up to One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) per Load. No released rates, tariff limitations or other limitation of cargo liability shall apply unless agreed in a writing signed by Ridgeline's General Counsel.

6.3 Claims shall be filed within nine (9) months of delivery. Carrier shall acknowledge each claim within thirty (30) days and shall pay, decline or make a firm settlement offer within one hundred twenty (120) days.

## 7. Indemnification

7.1 Each Party (the "Indemnifying Party") shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the other Party and its officers, employees and agents from and against any and all Claims to the extent arising out of the negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement by the Indemnifying Party or its employees, agents or subcontractors.

7.2 Without limiting Section 7.1, Carrier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Ridgeline from all Claims arising out of the operation, maintenance or use of Carrier's equipment or the acts or omissions of Carrier's drivers.

## 8. Insurance

8.1 Carrier shall maintain, at its sole expense, the following insurance with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:
(a) automobile liability insurance covering all owned, hired and non-owned vehicles with limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) combined single limit per occurrence;
(b) motor truck cargo insurance with limits of not less than One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) per Load; and
(c) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence.

8.2 Carrier shall cause its insurers to issue certificates of insurance naming Ridgeline Freight, LLC as certificate holder, and shall provide thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material change.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, special or consequential damages, including lost profits.

9.2 Nothing in this Section 9 limits (a) Carrier's liability for cargo under Section 6, (b) either Party's indemnification obligations under Section 7, or (c) either Party's liability for its own negligence or willful misconduct.

## 10. Non-Solicitation

10.1 During the Term and for twelve (12) months after the last Load tendered by Ridgeline to Carrier, Carrier shall not directly or indirectly solicit, or accept freight from, any Shipper whose freight Carrier first transported as a result of this Agreement, except through Ridgeline.

## 11. Term, Renewal and Termination

11.1 This Agreement has an initial term of one (1) year from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

11.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

11.3 Termination does not affect obligations for Loads tendered before the effective date of termination.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement, rates, and all Shipper information, and shall use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 13. Governing Law and Venue

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules, except to the extent preempted by federal law.

13.2 Any action arising out of this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Shelby County, Tennessee.

## 14. Miscellaneous

14.1 This Agreement, together with each Rate Confirmation, is the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements regarding its subject matter.

14.2 This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

14.3 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by courier or email to the addresses above.
