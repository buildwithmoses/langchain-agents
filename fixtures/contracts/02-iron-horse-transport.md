# MOTOR CARRIER TRANSPORTATION AGREEMENT

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Iron Horse Transport, Inc., Ridgeline Freight and all names, addresses and numbers herein are invented. Not legal advice; not a real contract.

**Carrier Form IHT-MCTA (Rev. 07/2026)**

THIS MOTOR CARRIER TRANSPORTATION AGREEMENT is made and entered into this 14th day of April, 2026, by and between **Iron Horse Transport, Inc.**, an Arkansas corporation (USDOT 0000002, MC-000002), 9 Example Industrial Drive, West Memphis, Arkansas 72300 ("Carrier"), and **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, Tennessee 38100 ("Broker").

WHEREAS, Carrier is a motor carrier authorized to transport general commodities in interstate commerce; and WHEREAS, Broker arranges for the transportation of property on behalf of its customers; NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the parties agree as follows:

## Article 1. Services

1.1 Carrier shall provide truckload transportation services for shipments tendered by Broker and accepted by Carrier. Each shipment shall be documented by a load confirmation sheet ("Load Confirmation") and a bill of lading.

1.2 Carrier retains the right to refuse any shipment. Carrier shall furnish properly maintained dry van and reefer equipment suitable for the commodity described in the Load Confirmation.

1.3 Carrier shall perform all services as an independent contractor. Carrier's drivers are employees or contractors of Carrier only.

## Article 2. Carrier Representations

2.1 Carrier holds active common or contract carrier authority and will maintain a safety rating of Satisfactory or Unrated.

2.2 Carrier shall comply with all federal and state laws applicable to its operations.

## Article 3. Compensation

3.1 Broker shall pay Carrier the rate stated in each Load Confirmation. Fuel surcharges, detention, layover and other accessorial charges are payable only as stated in the Load Confirmation.

3.2 Carrier shall submit its freight bill together with a clean signed delivery receipt. Broker shall remit payment within fifteen (15) days of receipt of Carrier's freight bill. Amounts not paid when due shall accrue a late charge of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month.

3.3 Carrier may offer an expedited payment program at its discretion.

## Article 4. Liability for Cargo

4.1 Carrier shall be responsible for loss or damage to cargo occurring while in Carrier's possession, subject to the limitations of this Article.

4.2 The rates charged by Carrier are released rates, established in reliance on the limitation of liability set forth herein. Carrier's maximum liability for loss of or damage to any shipment shall not exceed Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000) per shipment, unless a higher value is declared in writing by Broker prior to tender and Carrier agrees in writing to an excess value charge.

4.3 Carrier shall not be liable for loss or damage caused by an act of God, public enemy, act of the shipper, or inherent vice of the goods.

4.4 Claims must be submitted in writing within nine (9) months of delivery.

## Article 5. Indemnification

5.1 Broker shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Carrier, its officers, directors, employees and agents, from and against all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to the shipments tendered under this Agreement, including claims arising from inaccurate shipment information, improper loading by the shipper, or the acts or omissions of Broker or its customers.

5.2 The obligations in this Article shall survive termination of this Agreement.

## Article 6. Insurance

6.1 Carrier shall maintain the following insurance during the term of this Agreement:
- Auto liability: $1,000,000 combined single limit;
- Motor truck cargo: $100,000 per shipment;
- Commercial general liability: $1,000,000 per occurrence.

6.2 Carrier shall furnish Broker with certificates of insurance evidencing the coverage above, naming Ridgeline Freight, LLC as certificate holder, and shall update such certificates upon renewal of each policy.

## Article 7. Limitation of Damages

7.1 In no event shall either party be liable to the other for punitive, exemplary, special, incidental or consequential damages. This Article does not limit either party's liability for its own negligence or willful misconduct.

## Article 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 Carrier agrees that it will not solicit freight directly from any customer of Broker to whom Carrier was introduced by Broker, during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months following the last shipment handled by Carrier for Broker.

## Article 9. Term and Termination

9.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall remain in effect until terminated by either party.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party. Either party may terminate immediately upon a material breach that is not cured within ten (10) days after written notice.

## Article 10. Confidentiality

10.1 The parties shall keep the terms of this Agreement, including rates, confidential, except as required by law.

## Article 11. Governing Law; Venue

11.1 This Agreement shall be construed under the laws of the State of Tennessee and applicable federal law.

11.2 The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts sitting in Shelby County, Tennessee.

## Article 12. General Provisions

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the parties. Carrier's rules tariff is incorporated by reference only to the extent not inconsistent with this Agreement.

12.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.

12.3 Any modification must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.4 If any provision of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.
