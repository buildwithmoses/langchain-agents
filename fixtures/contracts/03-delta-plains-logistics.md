# CONTRACT CARRIER SERVICES AGREEMENT

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Delta Plains Logistics LLC, Ridgeline Freight and all names, addresses and numbers herein are invented. Not legal advice; not a real contract.

This Contract Carrier Services Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of May 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") between **Delta Plains Logistics LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (USDOT 0000003, MC-000003), with offices at 450 Sample Avenue, Greenville, Mississippi 38700 ("Carrier"), and **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, a Tennessee limited liability company, 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, Tennessee 38100 ("Broker").

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Broker arranges transportation of freight for shippers. Carrier provides contract carriage under 49 U.S.C. § 14101(b). The parties enter into this Agreement to set the terms on which Carrier will transport shipments arranged by Broker.

1.2 Each shipment shall be governed by this Agreement and a written dispatch order ("Dispatch Order"). This Agreement prevails over any conflicting Dispatch Order term.

## 2. Carrier Obligations

2.1 Carrier shall provide qualified drivers and safe, clean equipment; shall comply with all applicable safety regulations; and shall maintain a safety rating that is Satisfactory or Unrated.

2.2 Carrier shall provide check calls to Broker at least every four (4) hours while a shipment is in transit and shall notify Broker immediately of any delay, accident or cargo exception.

2.3 Carrier shall not broker, re-broker, subcontract or interline any shipment without Broker's prior written consent.

## 3. Rates and Invoicing

3.1 Carrier's rates are as stated in each Dispatch Order.

3.2 Broker shall pay Carrier's invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt of a complete invoice and signed proof of delivery. Carrier may elect quick pay at a fee of three percent (3%), with payment in two (2) business days.

## 4. Cargo Loss and Damage

4.1 Carrier assumes the liability of a motor carrier under 49 U.S.C. § 14706 for loss, damage or delay to cargo while in its care, custody or control.

4.2 Carrier shall be liable for the full actual value of lost or damaged cargo, not to exceed Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($250,000) per shipment. No released rate or tariff limitation shall reduce this liability.

4.3 Cargo claims shall be filed within nine (9) months after delivery.

## 5. Indemnity

5.1 Carrier shall indemnify, defend and hold Broker harmless from all claims, damages and expenses arising from Carrier's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.

5.2 Broker shall indemnify, defend and hold Carrier harmless from all claims, damages and expenses arising from Broker's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.

## 6. Insurance

6.1 Carrier shall procure and maintain: (a) automobile liability of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence; (b) cargo insurance of at least $100,000 per shipment; and (c) commercial general liability of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence.

6.2 All certificates of insurance shall name Ridgeline Freight, LLC as certificate holder and shall provide for thirty (30) days' advance notice of cancellation.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Except for indemnification obligations, cargo liability under Section 4, and liability for a party's own negligence or willful misconduct, neither party shall be liable for consequential, incidental or punitive damages.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 For the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after the last shipment tendered hereunder, Carrier shall not solicit or accept, directly or through an affiliate, any traffic from a shipper introduced to Carrier by Broker.

## 9. Term; Renewal; Termination

9.1 This Agreement shall have an initial term of two (2) years. Thereafter it shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either party delivers written notice of its intent not to renew no later than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by providing not less than ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other party.

9.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or loses its operating authority.

## 10. Independent Contractor

10.1 Carrier is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent or partner of Broker. Carrier is solely responsible for its drivers' wages, taxes and workers' compensation coverage.

## 11. Confidential Information

11.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's non-public business information, including customer lists and rates, during the term and for two (2) years thereafter.

## 12. Dispute Resolution; Governing Law

12.1 The parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through negotiation between executives for a period of thirty (30) days.

12.2 This Agreement and all claims arising under it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice-of-law principles.

12.3 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

## 13. Miscellaneous

13.1 Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice.

13.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement is the complete agreement of the parties and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

13.3 Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
