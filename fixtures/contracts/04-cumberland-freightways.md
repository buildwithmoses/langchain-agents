# BROKER-CARRIER AGREEMENT (CARRIER REDLINE v3)

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Cumberland Freightways Co., Ridgeline Freight and all names, addresses and numbers herein are invented. Not legal advice; not a real contract.

**Agreement No.** RF-CA-2026-0144 · **Effective Date:** June 9, 2026

This Broker-Carrier Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, a Tennessee limited liability company, 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, Tennessee 38100 ("Ridgeline"), and **Cumberland Freightways Co.**, a Tennessee corporation (USDOT 0000004, MC-000004), 75 Placeholder Pike, Nashville, Tennessee 37200 ("Carrier").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Customer" means any shipper, consignor or consignee for whom Ridgeline arranges transportation.

1.2 "Load" means a shipment tendered by Ridgeline and accepted by Carrier under a Rate Confirmation.

1.3 "Rate Confirmation" means Ridgeline's written tender for a Load.

1.4 "Losses" means all claims, damages, liabilities, fines, judgments, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 2. Engagement

2.1 Ridgeline may from time to time tender Loads to Carrier. Carrier may accept or decline each tender. Accepted Loads are governed by this Agreement and the Rate Confirmation, and this Agreement controls in the event of conflict.

2.2 Carrier is an independent contractor and shall have exclusive control of the manner and means of performing transportation.

## 3. Carrier Warranties

3.1 Carrier warrants that it holds valid operating authority, maintains a Satisfactory or Unrated safety rating, and has been in continuous operation for at least six (6) months.

3.2 Carrier shall not subcontract, re-broker or interline any Load.

3.3 Carrier shall comply with Ridgeline's tracking requirements, including check calls every four (4) hours while in transit.

## 4. Compensation

4.1 Rates are as set out in each Rate Confirmation. Accessorial charges (including detention after two (2) hours free time, and layover) are payable only if listed on the Rate Confirmation or approved in writing.

4.2 Ridgeline shall pay Carrier within thirty (30) days of receipt of Carrier's invoice and signed bill of lading. Carrier may request quick pay within two (2) days for a fee equal to three percent (3%) of the line haul.

## 5. Cargo Liability

5.1 Carrier shall have the liability of a motor carrier under the Carmack Amendment, 49 U.S.C. § 14706, for loss, damage or delay to cargo.

5.2 Carrier shall be liable for the full actual value of the cargo, up to One Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($150,000) per Load. Released rates shall not apply to any Load.

5.3 Cargo claims must be filed within nine (9) months after delivery.

## 6. Indemnification

6.1 Carrier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Ridgeline, its Customers, and their respective officers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of the negligent acts or omissions, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement by Carrier, its employees, drivers or agents.

6.2 Ridgeline shall indemnify Carrier from Losses to the extent caused by Ridgeline's own negligence.

## 7. Insurance

7.1 Carrier shall maintain, at its expense, with insurers authorized to do business in Tennessee:
(a) automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of not less than $1,000,000;
(b) motor truck cargo insurance with a limit of not less than Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000) per Load; and
(c) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence.

7.2 Carrier shall furnish certificates of insurance evidencing such coverage to Ridgeline upon request.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for lost profits or consequential damages. This limitation does not apply to Carrier's cargo liability, to indemnification obligations, or to a party's liability for its own negligence.

## 9. Non-Solicitation

9.1 Carrier shall not, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of six (6) months following the last Load handled by Carrier for Ridgeline, solicit or knowingly accept freight directly from any Customer to whom Carrier was introduced by Ridgeline.

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 The initial term of this Agreement is one (1) year. This Agreement automatically renews for additional one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of cancellation at least thirty (30) days before the renewal date.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 Carrier shall not disclose rates, Customer identities or Load information to any third party.

## 12. Governing Law and Venue

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee. Exclusive venue for any dispute lies in the state and federal courts of Shelby County, Tennessee.

## 13. General

13.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement and the Rate Confirmations constitute the entire agreement.

13.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

13.3 Severability. Any unenforceable provision shall be severed and the remainder enforced.

13.4 Notices. Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above.
