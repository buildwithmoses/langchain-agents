# TRANSPORTATION SERVICES AGREEMENT

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Red River Carriers, Inc., Ridgeline Freight and all names, addresses and numbers herein are invented. Not legal advice; not a real contract.

This Transportation Services Agreement (this "Agreement") dated July 20, 2026 is between **Red River Carriers, Inc.**, a Texas corporation (USDOT 0000005, MC-000005), 1200 Example Loop, Texarkana, Texas 75500 ("Carrier"), and **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, TN 38100 ("Broker").

## 1. Definitions

The following terms have the meanings set out below wherever used in this Agreement:

1.1 "Accessorials" means charges for services other than line haul transportation, including detention, layover, lumper and stop-off charges.

1.2 "Bill of Lading" means the receipt and contract of carriage issued for a Shipment.

1.3 "Charges" means the line haul rate and Accessorials payable for a Shipment.

1.4 "Maximum Cargo Value" means Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000) per Shipment, which the parties agree is the released value on which Carrier's rates are based.

1.5 "Shipment" means a quantity of freight tendered by Broker on one Bill of Lading.

1.6 "Tender" means Broker's written offer of a Shipment to Carrier.

## 2. Transportation Services

2.1 Carrier will transport Shipments tendered by Broker and accepted by Carrier, from origin to destination as stated in the Tender, with reasonable dispatch.

2.2 Carrier shall supply equipment in good repair and suitable for the commodity, including temperature-controlled equipment when specified.

2.3 Carrier is an independent contractor. Nothing herein creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

## 3. Charges and Payment

3.1 Broker shall pay Carrier the Charges stated in the Tender.

3.2 Carrier shall invoice Broker after delivery. Broker shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt of the invoice and a signed Bill of Lading. Quick pay within two (2) days is available for a three percent (3%) fee.

## 4. Cargo Liability

4.1 Carrier accepts liability for loss of or damage to cargo while in its possession in accordance with 49 U.S.C. § 14706.

4.2 Carrier's liability for loss or damage to any Shipment shall be limited to the lesser of (a) the actual value of the goods, or (b) the Maximum Cargo Value.

4.3 Claims shall be submitted within nine (9) months of delivery with supporting documentation.

## 5. Indemnification

5.1 Each party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other party from and against all third-party claims, losses and expenses, including attorneys' fees, to the extent caused by the indemnifying party's negligence or willful misconduct or its breach of this Agreement.

## 6. Insurance

6.1 Carrier shall maintain auto liability insurance of $1,000,000 combined single limit, motor truck cargo insurance of $100,000 per Shipment, and commercial general liability insurance of $1,000,000 per occurrence.

6.2 Carrier shall provide certificates of insurance naming Ridgeline Freight, LLC as certificate holder.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL CARRIER'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR ANY SHIPMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT OR OTHERWISE, AND INCLUDING LIABILITY ARISING FROM CARRIER'S OWN NEGLIGENCE, EXCEED THE TOTAL CHARGES PAID TO CARRIER FOR THE SHIPMENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

7.2 Neither party shall be liable for consequential, special or punitive damages.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 Carrier shall not solicit business directly from any shipper whose freight Carrier hauled under this Agreement for a period of twelve (12) months after the last Shipment for that shipper.

## 9. Term

9.1 The initial term of this Agreement is one (1) year. The Agreement will automatically renew for one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the current term.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of TN, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles, except where preempted by federal law. Venue for any suit shall be in Shelby County, TN.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 Assignment. Carrier may not assign this Agreement without Broker's written consent.

11.2 Waiver. No waiver of any breach shall be deemed a waiver of any later breach.

11.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement supersedes all prior understandings. Carrier's tariff does not apply except as stated in Section 1.4.

11.4 Survival. Sections 4, 5, 7 and 8 survive termination.
