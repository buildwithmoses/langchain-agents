**Agreement No.** RF-CA-2026-0189 · **Effective Date:** August 3, 2026

This Broker-Carrier Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, Tennessee 38100 ("Ridgeline"), and **Ozark Ridge Trucking LLC**, a Missouri limited liability company (USDOT 0000006, MC-000006), 8 Sample Hollow Road, Springfield, Missouri 65800 ("Carrier").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Customer" means any shipper or consignee for whom Ridgeline arranges transportation.

1.2 "Exhibits" means Exhibit A (Load Tender Procedures) and Exhibit B (Insurance Requirements), each of which is incorporated into this Agreement.

1.3 "Load" means freight tendered by Ridgeline and accepted by Carrier under a Rate Confirmation.

1.4 "Notice Period" means forty-five (45) days.

1.5 "Rate Confirmation" means Ridgeline's written tender for a Load, which may be delivered electronically.

1.6 "Losses" means all claims, liabilities, damages, costs, fines and reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 2. Services

2.1 Carrier shall transport Loads tendered by Ridgeline and accepted by Carrier in accordance with this Agreement, the Exhibits and the Rate Confirmation.

2.2 Carrier is an independent contractor and controls the means and methods of transportation.

2.3 Carrier shall follow the tender, tracking and documentation procedures in Exhibit A.

## 3. Carrier Compliance

3.1 Carrier shall maintain active operating authority and a Satisfactory or Unrated safety rating, and shall comply with all applicable laws.

3.2 Carrier shall not re-broker, subcontract or interline any Load and shall not transport hazardous materials.

## 4. Payment

4.1 Ridgeline shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt of the invoice and signed proof of delivery.

4.2 Carrier may elect quick pay in two (2) days for a fee of three percent (3%).

## 5. Cargo Liability

5.1 Carrier is liable for loss, damage or delay to cargo under the Carmack Amendment, 49 U.S.C. § 14706, for the full actual value of the cargo up to Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($200,000) per Load.

5.2 No released rates or tariff limitations apply. Claims shall be filed within nine (9) months of delivery.

## 6. Indemnity

6.1 Carrier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Ridgeline and its Customers from all Losses arising from the negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement by Carrier or its drivers, employees or agents.

## 7. Insurance

7.1 Carrier shall procure and maintain the insurance described in Exhibit B for the duration of this Agreement.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. This Section does not limit Carrier's liability for cargo, its indemnity obligations, or its liability for its own negligence.

## 9. Non-Solicitation

9.1 For the term and for twelve (12) months after the last Load, Carrier shall not solicit freight directly from any Customer introduced to Carrier by Ridgeline.

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 This Agreement has an initial term of one (1) year and renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the current term.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by written notice given to the other party not less than the Notice Period before the effective date of termination.

## 11. Governing Law and Venue

11.1 This Agreement is governed by Tennessee law. Venue lies exclusively in the courts of Shelby County, Tennessee.

## 12. General

12.1 This Agreement, the Exhibits and the Rate Confirmations are the entire agreement. Amendments must be in writing signed by both parties.

## Exhibit A – Load Tender Procedures

A.1 Ridgeline will issue a Rate Confirmation for each Load. Carrier shall sign and return the Rate Confirmation before dispatch.

A.2 Carrier shall provide check calls every four (4) hours while a Load is in transit and shall report any delay of more than two (2) hours immediately.

A.3 Carrier shall record arrival and departure times on the bill of lading at pickup and delivery. Detention is payable after two (2) hours of free time.

A.4 Carrier shall note all exceptions on the bill of lading at delivery and send photographs of any damage within twenty-four (24) hours.

## Exhibit B – Insurance Requirements

B.1 Automobile liability: not less than Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($750,000) combined single limit per occurrence, covering all owned, hired and non-owned vehicles.

B.2 Motor truck cargo: not less than One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) per Load.

B.3 Commercial general liability: not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence.

B.4 Certificates of insurance shall name Ridgeline Freight, LLC as certificate holder and require thirty (30) days' notice of cancellation.
