# BROKER-CARRIER AGREEMENT

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Tristar Express Lines, Inc., Ridgeline Freight and all names, addresses and numbers herein are invented. Not legal advice; not a real contract.

**Agreement No.** RF-CA-2026-0203 · **Effective Date:** September 14, 2026

This Broker-Carrier Agreement ("Agreement") is between **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, Tennessee 38100 ("Ridgeline"), and **Tristar Express Lines, Inc.**, a Georgia corporation (USDOT 0000007, MC-000007), 300 Example Commerce Blvd, Atlanta, Georgia 30300 ("Carrier").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Customer" means a shipper or consignee for whom Ridgeline arranges transportation.

1.2 "Load" means freight tendered by Ridgeline and accepted by Carrier.

1.3 "Losses" means claims, liabilities, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 2. Services and Relationship

2.1 Carrier shall transport Loads accepted under Rate Confirmations issued by Ridgeline. Carrier is an independent contractor.

2.2 Carrier shall maintain active operating authority, a Satisfactory or Unrated safety rating, and at least six (6) months of operating history.

## 3. Equipment and Drivers

3.1 Carrier shall furnish clean, safe and properly maintained equipment and qualified drivers.

3.2 For temperature-controlled Loads, Carrier shall pre-cool equipment and maintain the set point stated in the Rate Confirmation, and shall provide temperature download records on request.

## 4. Tracking

4.1 Carrier shall provide check calls every four (4) hours and immediate notice of any delay, accident or exception.

## 5. Rates and Payment

5.1 Rates are as stated in each Rate Confirmation.

5.2 Ridgeline shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt with a signed bill of lading. Quick pay within two (2) days is available for a three percent (3%) fee.

## 6. Cargo Liability

6.1 Carrier shall be liable under 49 U.S.C. § 14706 for the full actual value of lost or damaged cargo, up to One Hundred Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($125,000) per Load. Released rates do not apply.

6.2 Claims shall be filed within nine (9) months of delivery.

## 7. Indemnification

7.1 Carrier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Ridgeline and its Customers from all Losses arising out of Carrier's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement.

7.2 Ridgeline shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Carrier from all Losses arising out of Ridgeline's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement.

## 8. Insurance

8.1 Carrier shall maintain auto liability insurance of at least $1,000,000, cargo insurance of at least $100,000 per Load, and commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence.

8.2 Certificates shall name Ridgeline Freight, LLC as certificate holder.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither party is liable for consequential or punitive damages. Nothing in this Section limits Carrier's cargo liability, any indemnity obligation, or a party's liability for its own negligence.

## 10. Non-Solicitation

10.1 During the term and for twelve (12) months after the last Load, Carrier shall not solicit freight directly from any Customer introduced by Ridgeline.

## 11. Term and Termination

11.1 The term is one (1) year, renewing automatically for one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

11.2 Either party may terminate on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Carrier shall keep confidential all rates, Customer information and the terms of this Agreement.

## 13. Governing Law; Venue

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee.

13.2 Any litigation arising out of this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Davidson County, Tennessee.

## 14. Force Majeure

14.1 Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, road closures and government action.

## 15. Assignment

15.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a successor to substantially all of its business.

## 16. Miscellaneous

16.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement and the Rate Confirmations constitute the entire agreement of the parties.

16.2 Amendments. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

16.3 Notices. Notices shall be delivered in writing to the addresses above.

16.4 Allocation of Risk. Notwithstanding Section 7 or anything to the contrary in this Agreement, Carrier shall have no obligation to defend, indemnify or hold harmless Ridgeline or its Customers, and Section 7.1 shall be of no force or effect. Ridgeline's obligations under Section 7.2 shall remain in full force and shall survive termination.

16.5 Severability. If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest of this Agreement remains in effect.
