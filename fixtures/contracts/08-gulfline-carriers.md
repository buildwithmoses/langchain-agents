# MOTOR CARRIER AGREEMENT

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Gulfline Carriers LP, Ridgeline Freight and all names, addresses and numbers herein are invented. Not legal advice; not a real contract.

This Motor Carrier Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 5, 2026 by **Gulfline Carriers LP**, a Louisiana limited partnership (USDOT 0000008, MC-000008), 60 Example Wharf Road, Baton Rouge, Louisiana 70800 ("Carrier"), and **Ridgeline Freight, LLC**, 100 Example Parkway, Memphis, Tennessee 38100 ("Broker").

## 1. Scope

1.1 Carrier shall provide motor carrier services for freight tendered by Broker and accepted by Carrier. Rates and charges are set out in Exhibit A (Rate Schedule) and in individual load confirmations.

1.2 Exhibit A is incorporated into and forms part of this Agreement.

## 2. Carrier Status

2.1 Carrier is a for-hire motor carrier holding active FMCSA authority and is an independent contractor of Broker.

2.2 Carrier shall maintain a Satisfactory or Unrated safety rating and comply with all laws applicable to its operations.

## 3. Payment

3.1 Broker shall pay each correct invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt of the invoice and proof of delivery.

3.2 Carrier may, at its option, request quick pay within two (2) business days in exchange for a three percent (3%) discount.

## 4. Freight Loss and Damage

4.1 Carrier shall be liable for loss or damage to freight in its possession as provided in 49 U.S.C. § 14706, subject to the rates and terms of Exhibit A.

4.2 Claims must be filed within nine (9) months of delivery.

## 5. Indemnification

5.1 Carrier shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Broker and Broker's customers from and against all claims, losses, damages and expenses arising out of Carrier's negligence, willful misconduct, or failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

## 6. Insurance

6.1 Carrier shall maintain, at its own expense:
(a) automobile liability insurance with a limit of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence;
(b) motor truck cargo insurance with a limit of not less than $100,000 per shipment; and
(c) commercial general liability insurance with a limit of not less than Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000) per occurrence.

6.2 Carrier shall cause Ridgeline Freight, LLC to be named as certificate holder on all certificates of insurance.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Neither party shall be liable for any special, incidental or consequential damages. This Section does not apply to a party's liability for its own negligence, to Carrier's cargo liability, or to indemnification.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 Carrier shall not, during the term and for twelve (12) months following the last load tendered by Broker, solicit freight directly from any shipper introduced to Carrier by Broker.

## 9. Term and Termination

9.1 The initial term of this Agreement is one (1) year. Upon expiration, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives the other written notice of cancellation not less than sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 10. Governing Law and Venue

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee. Exclusive venue for any action lies in Shelby County, Tennessee.

## 11. General

11.1 This Agreement and Exhibit A are the entire agreement of the parties. Any amendment must be in writing signed by both parties.

11.2 Carrier may not assign this Agreement without Broker's prior written consent.

## Exhibit A – Rate Schedule

A.1 Line haul rates. Dry van: $2.85 per loaded mile. Reefer: $3.20 per loaded mile. Flatbed: $3.40 per loaded mile. Minimum charge per load: $650.

A.2 Fuel surcharge. Calculated weekly from the DOE national average diesel price using Carrier's published fuel table.

A.3 Accessorials. Detention after two (2) hours free time at $75 per hour; layover at $350 per day; stop-off at $100 per stop.

A.4 Released value. All rates in this Exhibit A are released rates. In consideration of these rates, Carrier's liability for loss of or damage to freight is limited to $2.50 per pound of the freight lost or damaged, not to exceed Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000) per shipment, unless Broker declares a higher value in writing and pays the excess valuation charge.

A.5 Rates in this Exhibit A may be adjusted by mutual written agreement.
