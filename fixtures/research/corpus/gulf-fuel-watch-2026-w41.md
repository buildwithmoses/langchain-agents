FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. Gulf Fuel Watch and these prices are invented for a demo.

The average retail on-highway diesel price in the Southeast was $3.94 per gallon in the week of October 6, 2026, up 31 cents from $3.63 at the start of July.

Reefer units add to the fuel bill: a refrigeration unit burns roughly 0.8 gallons of diesel per hour while cooling, so a 30-hour produce run can use about 24 extra gallons beyond the tractor's fuel.

Under a typical broker fuel surcharge table that adds one cent per mile for every six-cent rise in diesel, the 31-cent increase since July works out to roughly five cents more per mile in surcharge.
