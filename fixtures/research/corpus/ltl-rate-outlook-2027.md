FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. The publisher and these figures are invented for a demo.

Five of the largest LTL carriers have announced general rate increases (GRIs) for early 2027 ranging from 4.9% to 6.5%, with a median of 5.9%.

Accessorial charges are rising faster than base rates. Liftgate fees are going up by an average of 9%, and two carriers are adding a new residential delivery surcharge.

Shippers that tender fewer than 50 LTL shipments a month usually see the full GRI, while larger shippers typically negotiate it down by one to three points.
