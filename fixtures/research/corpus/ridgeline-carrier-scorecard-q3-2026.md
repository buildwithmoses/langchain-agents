FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. Ridgeline Freight is fictional and so are these numbers.

On-time pickup across all Ridgeline loads was 93.1% in Q3 2026, down from 95.4% in Q2. On-time delivery was 91.8%, down from 94.0%.

Reefer was the weakest mode: reefer on-time delivery fell to 87.2% in Q3 from 92.5% in Q2, driven by the September hurricane disruption and longer produce dwell times.

Carrier fall-off (a carrier cancelling after accepting a load) rose to 4.4% of reefer loads in Q3, compared with 2.1% in Q2.

Carrier Relations removed 37 carriers from the approved list in Q3, mostly for lapsed insurance.
