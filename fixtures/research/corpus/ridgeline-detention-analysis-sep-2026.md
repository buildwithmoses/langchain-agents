FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. Ridgeline Freight and every shipper named here are fictional.

In September 2026 Ridgeline paid $41,800 in detention to carriers on produce loads, up from $23,500 in June. Under our policy carriers get 2 free hours, then $75 per hour capped at $600 per stop.

Three produce shippers accounted for 58% of the September detention bill:

- Okefenokee Fresh Farms (Valdosta, GA): average dwell of 4.6 hours per pickup, $11,200 in detention.
- Sunbelt Pepper Co. (Tifton, GA): average dwell of 3.9 hours per pickup, $7,900 in detention.
- Gator Grove Citrus (Lakeland, FL): average dwell of 3.4 hours per pickup, $5,150 in detention.

Only 62% of September produce detention was billed back to the shipper, because the rest lacked signed in and out times on the BOL.

Recommendation: require appointment confirmations from these three shippers and enforce signed BOL in/out times before paying detention.
